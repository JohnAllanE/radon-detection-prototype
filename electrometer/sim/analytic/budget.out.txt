== Ranges (R-01, R-07) ==
Integrator: charge per ramp = Cf x swing = 40 pC; max current at 10 ms per ramp = 4 nA
R1 = 3.9 Mohm: full scale 1.03 uA; offset before zeroing 10.3 pA; offset drift 128 fA/K; lowest reading at 1 % of reading after zeroing ~ 12.8 pA per K of drift
R2 = 1 Gohm: full scale 4 nA; offset before zeroing 40 fA; offset drift 500 aA/K; lowest reading at 1 % of reading after zeroing ~ 50 fA per K of drift
Coverage: integrator 10 fA..~4 nA, R2 ~4 pA..3.9 nA (continuous), R1 ~1 nA..1 uA. Overlap with the SDM3055 200 uA range: 100 nA..1 uA (R-07).

== Input capacitance and noise gain ==
PD bias on (20 V): Cin = 102 pF, noise gain (integrator) = 1 + Cin/Cf = 11.2
PD bias 0 V (fA runs): Cin = 219 pF, noise gain (integrator) = 1 + Cin/Cf = 22.9

== Integrator current resolution, op-amp limited (R-01 target 10 fA) ==
  PD bias on, T =    1 s: from en 448 aApp, from in 327 aApp, total ~ 554 aApp
  PD bias on, T =   10 s: from en 44.8 aApp, from in 103 aApp, total ~ 113 aApp
  PD bias 0 V, T =    1 s: from en 916 aApp, from in 327 aApp, total ~ 973 aApp
  PD bias 0 V, T =   10 s: from en 91.6 aApp, from in 103 aApp, total ~ 138 aApp
Conclusion: the amplifier is not the limit for 10 fA; leakage, triboelectric charge, dielectric absorption and humidity are (risks R1-R4 in the architecture).

== Resistor ranges: thermal noise of Rf (1 s reading, ENBW 0.5 Hz) ==
R1: 65 fA/rtHz -> 45.9 fA rms per 1 s reading
R2: 4.06 fA/rtHz -> 2.87 fA rms per 1 s reading

== Loop stability, transimpedance ranges (worst case Cin) ==
R1: noise-gain zero 178 Hz, pole 4.08 kHz, HF noise gain 22.9, crossover 87.3 kHz, phase margin ~87 deg; signal bandwidth 4.08 kHz
R2: noise-gain zero 695 mHz, pole 15.9 Hz, HF noise gain 22.9, crossover 87.3 kHz, phase margin ~90 deg; signal bandwidth 15.9 Hz
Both ranges have more than 60 deg margin; a SPICE check (S-01) confirms with the real model.

== Mains rejection (Q-08) ==
A slope or average over a multiple of 100 ms spans whole cycles of both 50 Hz (5) and 60 Hz (6), and a delta-sigma ADC with simultaneous 50/60 Hz rejection does the same for the resistor ranges, so the design does not need to know the mains frequency.

== Leakage at the summing node (everything has about Vos across it) ==
  40 uV across 1 Tohm -> 40 aA
  40 uV across 100 Tohm -> 4e-19 A
  Op-amp input bias max 20 fA (zeroed; the typical value at 25 C is far lower)
  Open Sa contacts, clamp diodes, the reset switch's summing-node side and C_inj's drive line all sit at ~0 V, so their leakage scales with Vos, not with the 4 V output or the 50 V source.

== Calibration currents from the injection capacitor (architecture §3.1) ==
  C_inj x ramp 0.01 V/s = 10 fA
  C_inj x ramp 0.1 V/s = 100 fA
  C_inj x ramp 1 V/s = 1 pA
  C_inj x ramp 4 V/s = 4 pA

== Input protection vs burden (R-01, R-10) ==
  Series R 10 kohm: burden 10 mV at 1 uA, 40 uV at 4 nA; thermal noise 12.8 nV/rtHz (adds to en)
  Series R 100 kohm: burden 100 mV at 1 uA, 400 uV at 4 nA; thermal noise 40.6 nV/rtHz (adds to en)
  Fault current from a 1 kV flashover is set by the HV board's output resistance: at 10 Mohm it is 100 uA, which clamp diodes and a 10 kohm series R handle easily (requirement on the HV board).

== Why the resistor check is an external cal plug, not a resistor fixed to the summing node (R-25) ==
  1 Gohm left on the node: thermal noise 4.06 fA/rtHz (2.87 fA rms in a 1 s reading) and 10 pA from a source 'at 0 V' that is 10 mV off, against the 10 fA target
  100 Gohm left on the node: thermal noise 406 aA/rtHz (287 aA rms in a 1 s reading) and 100 fA from a source 'at 0 V' that is 10 mV off, against the 10 fA target
  C_inj injects only C*dV/dt: a static DAC offset gives no current, and an ideal capacitor has no thermal noise.

== Source DAC, coarse + fine (R-08: 10 mV steps) ==
  12-bit alone: 24.4 mV per step
  coarse + fine (fine full scale = 4 coarse LSB): 23.8 uV per step, about 22 bits; monotonic and accurate only through the ADC readback (R-21)
