## Question 1: which pipe size to build the tubes from

**The finding.** One die per tube beats the four-die array in every tube of 1/2 in and up, because the ions funnel to the axis where the single die sits. The share of the gas volume that lands on the active area peaks at 83–90 % in 1/2 to 3/4 in tubes; the interaction index ($ID^2 f$, the count rate per detector up to a constant) keeps rising with size, but slowly past 1-1/4 in.

| Option (top copper +200 V) | Share to active area | $ID^2 f$ (mm²) | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) 3/4 in sch 40, today's pipe | 83 % | 362 | None new; half the counts per channel of (b) |
| (b) 1-1/4 in sch 40 | 56 % | 684 | New pipe, crosses and flanges; a board about 42 mm OD plus the flange (size `VERIFY`) |
| (c) 1-1/2 in sch 40 | 44 % | 733 | Larger still, 7 % over (b) |

Schedule 10S is too thin to thread reliably (S-02, pipe sizes) and 1/4 in can't hold the 5.67 mm die.

**Recommendation:** (b). Counts per channel set how long a run takes (days per 100 counts indoors, `chamber-sensitivity.md`), and each channel costs a CSA, so the index per detector is the figure to buy. Keep (a) for a first test if the 3/4 in pipe is already on hand: the board design is the same, only its outline changes.

## Question 2: a top-copper bias above 200 V

**The finding.** In the 1-1/4 in tube, raising $V_{top}$ moves ions off the copper onto the die: 41 % at 0 V, 56 % at 200 V, 67 % at 400 V, 76 % at 600 V. But the copper's inner edge is 0.3 mm from the die at 0 V, so its field rises with the bias: 0.85 kV/mm at 200 V, 2.6 kV/mm at 600 V (50 µm grid), against roughly 3 kV/mm for air (`VERIFY`). The copper also drives leakage across that gap into the CSA input.

| Option | Gain over 200 V | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| (a) +200 V from the HV board's transformer stage (A-20) | none | An RC filter per board |
| (b) +400 V | 11 points of share, $ID^2 f$ 684 → 817 | A separate tap or divider on the HV board, a wider die-to-copper gap (simulate again), more guard |
| (c) +600 V | 20 points | As (b), and the edge field leaves almost no margin |

**Recommendation:** (a) now. Lay out the copper so a wider gap is possible, and try 400 V later if the count rate disappoints.
